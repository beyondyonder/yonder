Yonder Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026 (testing version)

Yonder recommends movies and shows for you, and for you and the people you watch with. This policy explains what Yonder collects, why, and the choices you have. Yonder is in testing, and this policy will be updated before it is publicly available.

What Yonder collects

  • Your first name, as you enter it, so each profile on a phone can be told apart.
  • Your answers and ratings: how you answered each question card (including "haven't seen it") and every rating you give.
  • How you use the app: which titles were recommended to you, which you rated, marked not interested, or scrolled past, which screen each action happened on, and when.
  • Technical details: the app version, and a random identifier created for your phone. This identifier is not linked to your Apple ID, phone number or any other app.

Yonder does not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifiers.

How it is collected

Everything above is stored on your phone. While Share my results with Yonder is on (it is on unless you turn it off in Profile), the app also sends a copy to Yonder from time to time: when you finish the questions, when you leave the app after making changes, and when you open it (no more than once a day). Copies are stored in Apple's iCloud service on Yonder's behalf, and only Yonder can read them.

You can also send your results yourself with Manually email results to Yonder in Profile.

If you share your profile link or QR code with someone (for example, to see recommendations together), it contains your first name and your ratings. Only share it with people you choose.

How Yonder uses it

  • To make and improve recommendations, for you and for everyone who uses Yonder.
  • To measure how accurate recommendations are and to find and fix problems.

Yonder does not sell your information, does not show ads, and does not use your information to track you across other companies' apps or websites. It is not shared with anyone else, except Apple, which stores it.

Your choices

  • Turn sharing off at any time in Profile. Nothing more is sent automatically after that. What you've already sent is kept unless you ask for it to be deleted.
  • Delete your data: email the address below with your first name and roughly when you used Yonder, and Yonder will delete what it has received from you. Removing a profile from the app deletes it from your phone.

How long it is kept

Yonder keeps shared results to keep improving recommendations, unless you ask for them to be deleted. First names are removed from shared results within one year after the testing period ends.

Children

Yonder is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: yondersupport@icloud.com
