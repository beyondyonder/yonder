Yonder Support

Yonder recommends movies and shows for you, and for you and the people you watch with.

Contact

Questions, problems, or a request to delete your data: email yondersupport@icloud.com.

Common questions

How do I stop sharing my results? In the app, open Profile and turn off Share my results with Yonder.

How do I get better recommendations? Rate titles you've seen from your lists, or tap Answer more questions at the end of a list.

How do I watch with someone? Share your code from the Users tab, and add theirs on the Together tab.
